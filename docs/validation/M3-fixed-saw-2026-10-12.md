## Implemented behavior

Eight preallocated PolyBLEP saw voices, equal temperament with A4=440 Hz, linear
MIDI velocity, fixed 0.1 gain per voice, and identical stereo channels. Internal
linear fades are 3 ms attack and 5 ms release, rounded up to whole samples.
No effects, filters, parameters, or adjustable amplitude controls were added.

MIDI note-on/off is handled at event sample positions. Repeated pitches use
separate slots; note-off releases the oldest surviving held/queued matching
pitch on its channel. Zero-velocity note-on is note-off. Full allocation prefers
a releasing slot, then the oldest held voice, then the oldest queued replacement.
Stealing fades the old voice for at most 1 ms before starting its replacement.
Cancelling/replacing a queued note cannot prolong the fade or revive a dropped
note. CC123 releases/cancels notes on its channel; CC120 silences it immediately.
Prepare, release, and reset clear all notes. Sustain/bend/modulation are deferred.

Plugin name/vendor/version remain LAZ-SYNTH / LAZ / 0.1.0. Component/controller
CIDs remain `ABCDEF019182FAEB4C617A6153796261` and
`ABCDEF011234ABCD4C617A6153796261`. Live voices are not persisted.
